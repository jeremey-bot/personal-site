  1. Tap Copy signature.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature. Choose Per Account if you only want it on your personal address.
  3. Clear the old signature, then touch and hold in the field and tap Paste.
  4. If the blue square or the colors disappear, shake the iPhone straight away and tap Undo Change Attributes.
  5. Send yourself a test email.
