Expertise

Sweetness touches cost, labeling, supply, safety and taste at once. That makes it one of the clearest places where an ingredient decision becomes a business decision. These are the areas where my experience is most useful.

Sweetener systems and sugar reduction

The situation
A brand needs less sugar without losing what people buy it for.
The decisions
How much nutritive sweetener and total sweetness to target, which blend gets there, and what it does to cost, claims and the plant.
My experience
Led formulation for Capri Sun’s 40% sugar reduction, using designed experiments to fix an inherited formula that missed its sensory and cost targets.

High-intensity sweeteners and rare sugars

The situation
Stevia, Reb M, monk fruit, allulose and tagatose each solve different problems, with different supply, cost and label implications.
The decisions
Which sweetener fits the claim, the budget and the process.
My experience
Technical evaluation and sample development behind ASR Group’s partnerships with Amyris (Reb M by fermentation) and Bonumose (tagatose); monk fruit at commercial scale for Capri Sun; today, a new high-intensity sweetener.

Experimental design for formulation

The situation
Reformulations stall in rounds of trial and error.
The decisions
How to structure experiments so cost, sensory and processing constraints are optimized together.
My experience
Used designed experiments to optimize the Capri Sun formula. At Kraft Heinz I helped run a five-person R&D statistics group that trained about 25 colleagues in statistics and experimental design.
