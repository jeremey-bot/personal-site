About

I got into food science because I love food and what it does for people: the way a meal or a drink can make someone feel. I studied food science at the University of Illinois and started my career at ASR Group, the cane sugar refiner, just as sweeteners were becoming one of the industry’s biggest questions. They still are.

At Kraft Heinz I spent six years in R&D. I led the formulation behind Capri Sun’s 40% sugar reduction, took MiO’s Big Bottle from concept to launch, and worked on sweetener technology strategy. I also helped run a statistics group that taught colleagues experimental design, and built the R&D request-tracking system that earned a Golden Noodle Award.

After working on beverage development at OFI, I moved to the commercial side to see the industry from another angle. I now bring a new sweetener to market at MycoTechnology. Explaining technical work to non-technical people turns out to be the skill I use most.

I live in Chicago, where I cook as often as I can and still follow Illinois basketball.
