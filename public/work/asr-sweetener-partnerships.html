New sweeteners, from evaluation to partnership

At ASR Group, one of the world’s largest cane sugar refiners, I worked on the company’s move into alternative sweeteners.

Context

I worked on ASR Group’s Sugar Optimization initiative as the company expanded beyond sugar into alternative sweeteners.

Challenge and constraints

The new sweeteners were early stage. When I began evaluating Bonumose’s tagatose, the company couldn’t yet produce saleable product.

My role

  • Technical evaluation, demo and sample development, and commercial marketing materials for both partnerships.
  • Early customer prospecting for Bonumose, before any formal partnership existed.
  • Executive presentations that secured internal buy-in for external partnerships.

Outcome

  • In 2018, ASR became the first commercial customer and long-term partner for Amyris’s Reb M, made by fermentation.
  • In 2021, ASR co-led Bonumose’s Series B with Hershey and became its distribution partner. Tagatose shipments followed in 2023.

The lesson

A new sweetener is sold through samples and applications, not spec sheets. At that stage, the technical work is the commercial work.
