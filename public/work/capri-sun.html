Capri Sun: 40% less sugar

The largest Capri Sun renovation at Kraft Heinz cut sugar by an average of 40% across the entire original pouch line. I led the formulation.

Context

Capri Sun is the leading kids’ juice drink brand, licensed by Kraft Heinz in the U.S. Parents were increasingly concerned about sugar, and Kraft Heinz had committed to removing more than 60 million pounds of sugar from its portfolio by 2025.

Challenge and constraints

  • The formula I inherited didn’t meet sensory or cost standards.
  • The brand had to keep its “all natural ingredients” claim, and marketing ruled out stevia.
  • The change had to run at full scale, across every flavor.

My role

I owned formulation, ingredient evaluation and the designed experiments used to optimize the formula, through bench and pilot-plant production to first production at the three Kraft Heinz Capri Sun facilities. Monk fruit was chosen jointly by marketing, procurement, R&D and senior leadership.

Approach and decisions

Rather than iterate by trial and error, I used designed experiments to optimize the formula within sensory and cost limits at the same time.

The new formula was validated through sensory testing with kids, cost sign-off, and parent evaluation of the label and sugar content.

Outcome

  • An average of 40% less sugar across the original pouch line: about 8 g total and 5 g added sugar per pouch, down from up to 13 g and 11 g.
  • On shelves in August 2022, described by Kraft Heinz as the largest renovation in Capri Sun’s history at the company.
  • Expected to deliver more than half of Kraft Heinz’s 60-million-pound sugar goal, according to Food Dive.
  • Kraft Heinz’s CEO noted the switch also reduced exposure to volatile sugar markets.

The lesson

The sweetener choice was a brand, procurement and plant decision as much as a technical one. Getting it right meant satisfying all three at once.
