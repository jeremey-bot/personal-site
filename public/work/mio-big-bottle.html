MiO Big Bottle

A double-size, multi-million-dollar line extension of Kraft Heinz’s liquid water enhancer. I led it from concept to launch in 2020.

Context

MiO is Kraft Heinz’s liquid water enhancer. The Big Bottle doubled the size of the original. It was one of the first projects I managed.

Challenge and constraints

Doubling the bottle also doubled the caffeine in each bottle of the MiO Energy line. That reopened a safety question that had been settled carefully when MiO first launched, and it needed internal regulatory approval.

My role

I led the product from concept to launch, including the safety case and the post-launch monitoring program.

Approach and decisions

The case for moving ahead rested on safeguards already built into the product: a dosing nozzle in the packaging, a concentrated acidity that makes it unpleasant to drink undiluted, and MiO’s track record.

To go further, I set up third-party safety monitoring after launch, both to help anyone who over-consumed and to measure whether it was happening at all.

Outcome

The Big Bottle launched in 2020. After two years on the market with no complaints to the third-party monitor, monitoring moved into Kraft Heinz’s standard consumer-response channels.

The lesson

The strongest safety case is the one you can prove after launch. Build the evidence-gathering into the plan.
